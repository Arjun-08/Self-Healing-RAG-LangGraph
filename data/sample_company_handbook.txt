Acme Analytics Company Handbook

About the company
Acme Analytics is a fictional analytics company used for demonstration of the Self-Healing RAG system. The company was founded in 2018 and is headquartered in Bengaluru.

Engineering practices
Production services use automated unit tests, integration tests, and deployment checks. Critical services require two reviewers before production deployment. Engineers are expected to document major architectural decisions.

Incident response
For a production incident, the on-call engineer first acknowledges the alert, assesses severity, and creates an incident record. Severity-1 incidents require an incident commander. After mitigation, the team writes a post-incident review within five business days.

Security
Customer data must not be copied into personal storage. Access to production systems follows least-privilege principles. Secrets must be stored in the approved secrets manager rather than source code.

Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Production incidents are an exception: on-call engineers may be required to respond outside normal working hours.

Performance reviews
Performance reviews occur twice per year. Engineers are evaluated on technical impact, reliability, collaboration, and mentorship.

Learning budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 500 USD for approved courses, books, and conferences.
