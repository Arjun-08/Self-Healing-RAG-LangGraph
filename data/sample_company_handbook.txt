ACME ANALYTICS — COMPANY HANDBOOK

COMPANY
Acme Analytics was founded in 2018. The company builds analytics software for enterprise customers and operates engineering, security, product, and customer-success teams.

REMOTE WORK
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Teams may establish additional collaboration days when project requirements justify them.

PRODUCTION DEPLOYMENTS
Critical services require two reviewers before production deployment. The deployment owner must verify that required tests pass and that the deployment record is complete.

INCIDENT RESPONSE
Critical incidents must be acknowledged promptly by the on-call engineer. After mitigation, the incident owner must write a post-incident review within five business days. The review should document impact, timeline, root cause, contributing factors, and corrective actions.

SECRETS MANAGEMENT
Production secrets must be stored in the approved secrets manager rather than source code, tickets, or chat messages. Credentials should be rotated when exposure is suspected.

LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT
Employees may receive an annual learning budget of up to 1500 dollars for approved courses, conferences, books, and technical training.

SECURITY INCIDENTS
If a production credential is accidentally exposed, the employee must immediately report the exposure to the security team and rotate the credential. Security may require additional credential rotation or access review.

AUDIT LOGGING
Standard audit logs are retained for 180 days. Certain regulated workloads may have longer retention requirements defined by their service owner.

CUSTOMER DATA
Production customer data must not be copied into development environments. Engineers should use sanitized or synthetic data for development and testing. Exceptions require explicit security approval.

MEETING GUIDELINES
Teams are encouraged to keep recurring meetings focused and to document decisions in the appropriate project workspace. Meeting guidelines do not override security or production-change requirements.

EXPENSES
Routine business expenses require receipts according to the finance policy. Travel approvals are handled through the normal company workflow.
